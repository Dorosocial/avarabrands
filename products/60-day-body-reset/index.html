How This Program Works
No fads, no fear-mongering — just targeted, research-backed movements for the things you've actually asked about. Fifteen minutes a day, most days, for 60 days.
1

Check off days, log how you feel, and revisit measurements every two weeks — not the scale.

Inner Thigh
Copenhagen Plank
An unconventional but heavily studied isometric hold that isolates the inner thigh far more directly than squats or lunges.
3Sets20 secHoldEach sideReps
1Stack your hips directly on top of each other, top shin resting on the bench.
2Brace through your core and hold the position — no swinging or sinking.
3Breathe steadily throughout the hold; tension stays constant, not explosive.
Easier
Rest your top foot on a low step or pillow.
Harder
Add a resistance band above the knees.

Wall Angels
A slow, controlled pattern that retrains your shoulder blades to move the way they're supposed to — the single best fix for a rounded, desk-shaped upper back.
3Sets12RepsSlowTempo
1Stand with your head, upper back and tailbone against the wall.
2Slide your arms up slowly, keeping every point of contact.
3If a point lifts off the wall, that's your new range — stop there.
Easier

Harder
Hold two seconds at the top of every rep.

Page 244FAQ & safety disclaimer
60-DAY BODY RESETFAQ & Safety
Questions & Safety
Will this actually change how my face looks?
Facial yoga has early but promising evidence for muscle tone; think of it as a bonus habit, not a miracle. Consistency over 60 days is what compounds.
I felt it in my lower back during Copenhagen planks — normal?
Mild new-muscle soreness is normal for 1–2 days. Sharp or joint pain is not — switch to the easier modification and stop if it persists.
Can I do this while pregnant or postpartum?
Many moves are gentle, but check with your provider first — especially anything in the Core + Waist and Inner Thigh programs.
What if I miss a day?
Pick up where you left off. The plan is 60 days of movement, not 60 consecutive days — life happens.
Always stop if
You feel sharp or shooting pain, dizziness, or chest tightness. This program is educational and not a substitute for medical advice.
